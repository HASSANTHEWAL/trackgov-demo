How it works

  1. Install

    A tracking device is fitted to each government vehicle.

  2. Transmit

    Position, speed and fuel readings are sent to the TrackGov server.

  3. Monitor

    Vehicles, routes and alerts appear together in one dashboard.

  4. Report

    Trips and fuel use are compiled into reports for review.
